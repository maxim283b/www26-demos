### 4 Conclusion

In this paper, we have studied the problem of monotone submodular maximization under a knapsack constraint and aim to find a deterministic combinatorial algorithm with subquadratic query complexity and approximation ratio strictly larger than <sup>1</sup>/2. In order to investigate this problem, we introduce a structural parameter called cost ratio. With this tool, we firstly prove that when ⌊��/��⌋ = 1, no deterministic combinatorial algorithm with subquadratic query complexity can achieve an approximation ratio �� <sup>&</sup>gt; <sup>1</sup>/2.
